```python
Ultraviolet to infrared dielectric function of ultrathin VO2 films grown by atomic layer deposition

```

你在论文里最好同时说明两层来源：

第一层是**论文来源**，也就是这些 VO₂ 光学常数是谁测的、怎么测的；第二层是**原始数据文件来源**，也就是你实际下载的 insulating / metallic CSV 来自哪里。

对你现在采用的这套数据，主参考文献就是：

**D. Louisos, M. McLamb, J. Engeland, V. Paige Stinson, N. Shuchi, G. Boreman, and T. Hofmann, “Ultraviolet to infrared dielectric function of ultrathin VO₂ films grown by atomic layer deposition,” Optical Materials Express, 16(4), 1115–1126 (2026). DOI: 10.1364/OME.591297.** 论文采用变角光谱椭偏测量并建立 Kramers–Kronig 一致的介电函数模型，覆盖 0.045–5.9 eV；样品是 ALD 生长并退火结晶的超薄 VO₂，名义厚度约 36 nm。([New Jersey Institute of Technology](https://researchwith.njit.edu/en/publications/ultraviolet-to-infrared-dielectric-function-of-ultrathin-vosub2su/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

更重要的是，论文正文明确说明：

> tabulated n,kn,k 数据作为 Data Files 提供，**Data File 1 对应 insulating VO₂，Data File 2 对应 metallic VO₂**。([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/402243277_Ultraviolet_to_infrared_dielectric_function_of_ultrathin_VO2_films_grown_by_atomic_layer_deposition))

所以你实际使用的两个数据文件也可以单独追溯：

- `VO2_Insulating.csv`：Figshare DOI **10.6084/m9.figshare.31558441**，页面明确写的是 “Optical constants of VO₂ in its insulating phase.” ([Optica Publishing](https://opticapublishing.figshare.com/articles/dataset/VO2_Insulating_csv/31558441))
- `VO2_Metallic.csv`：Figshare DOI **10.6084/m9.figshare.31558459**，页面明确写的是 “Optical constants of VO₂ in its metallic phase.” ([Optica Publishing](https://opticapublishing.figshare.com/articles/dataset/VO2_Metallic_csv/31558459))

你论文 Methods 里可以直接这样写：

> The wavelength-dependent complex refractive indices of VO₂ in the insulating and metallic states were taken from the spectroscopic-ellipsometry dataset reported by Louisos *et al.* The VO₂ films were deposited by atomic layer deposition and had a nominal thickness of approximately 36 nm. The tabulated optical constants corresponding to the insulating and metallic phases were obtained from Data Files 1 and 2, respectively. ([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/402243277_Ultraviolet_to_infrared_dielectric_function_of_ultrathin_VO2_films_grown_by_atomic_layer_deposition))

如果写成中文：

> VO₂ 绝缘态和金属态的波长相关复折射率 n(λ)+ik(λ)n(\lambda)+ik(\lambda) 采用 Louisos 等人报道的实验光学常数。该数据通过变角光谱椭偏方法获得，对应 ALD 生长并经退火结晶的约 36 nm VO₂ 薄膜。本文分别采用其 Data File 1 和 Data File 2 中给出的绝缘态和金属态 n,kn,k 数据进行光学仿真。([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/402243277_Ultraviolet_to_infrared_dielectric_function_of_ultrathin_VO2_films_grown_by_atomic_layer_deposition))
